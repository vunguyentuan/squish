Drop to Optimize

Quietly brilliant.

Feels like it came with your Mac. Does more than it lets on.

🎞️

Videos too

MOV and MP4 re-encoded to HEVC on Apple's hardware encoder. Resolution, rotation, HDR and audio are kept, and the result starts playing instantly on the web.

🛟

Safe by default

Originals go to the Trash, so Restore is one click away. Finder tags and dates are preserved, and optimizing never makes a file bigger.

🎯

Pixel-perfect mode

The Lossless preset shrinks PNG and JPEG files without changing a single pixel. Balanced and Smallest go further when you want them to.

🔁

Convert in a click

Turn HEIC, TIFF, PNG or JPEG into WebP, AVIF or JPEG. Compare before and after with a slider, or press Space for Quick Look.

🧭

Everywhere you work

Drop on the window, the Dock icon or the shelf, use Finder's Services menu, or keep it in the menu bar. Folders are scanned for you.

🔒

Private and native

A SwiftUI app with a Rust engine. No accounts, no uploads, no analytics. Your files never leave your Mac.

The best encoders in town.

Squish picks the strongest open-source codec for each format and tunes it, so you don't have to.

FormatWhat Squish does
PNGlibimagequant palette quantization (the engine behind pngquant), then oxipng's lossless Deflate search
JPEGmozjpeg trellis re-encode, or a lossless jpegtran-style transcode, whichever is smaller
WebPlibwebp with sharp YUV, up to its slowest, strongest method
AVIFrav1e AV1 encoder via ravif
SVGOXVG, a Rust port of SVGO
VideoHEVC on Apple's hardware encoder, HDR stays 10-bit
