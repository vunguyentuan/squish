- **Resize to fit.** Set a maximum size (e.g. 2560 px) in the toolbar options and larger images and videos are scaled down as they're optimized.
- **Watched folders.** Settings → Automation: new files saved to a folder are optimized automatically. One click watches your Screenshots folder.
- **Optimize the clipboard.** Press ⌃⌥⌘V to shrink a copied screenshot before you paste it into Slack, GitHub or Mail.
- **Animated GIFs.** Convert them to animated WebP or to MP4 video, usually 80–95% smaller.
- **Per-format rules.** Settings → Formats: keep PNGs lossless, turn HEIC into JPEG, send GIFs to MP4, each with its own preset.
- **Shortcuts.** New "Optimize Files" and "Optimize Clipboard Image" actions for the Shortcuts app, Spotlight and Siri.
- **Command line.** Install the `squish` tool from Settings → Automation (`squish --help`).
- **Reports.** Copy a summary (⇧⌘C) or export a CSV (⌘E) of a batch, and see how much Squish has saved you all time.
- Squish is now available in Vietnamese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.
